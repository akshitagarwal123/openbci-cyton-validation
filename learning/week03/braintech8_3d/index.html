The board we plan to build, in 3D

An 8-channel EEG board: one ADS1299, an ESP32-S3 with Wi-Fi, a LiPo with USB-C charging, on a 4-layer board of about 40 × 35 mm. Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, click a part to see what it does and why it sits where it does.

Concept placement for learning, not a routed layout. Part sizes are from typical datasheets; positions, the electrode connector's pin count and the Layer 3 power areas are proposals to check during the KiCad layout (week 4).

How this layout follows the lessons

Stack-up: Top (parts + fast signals) · Layer 2 one solid ground · Layer 3 power areas (+2.5 V, −2.5 V, 3.3 V, battery) · Bottom (parts + slow signals). Every top-layer trace has unbroken ground about 0.2 mm below it.

Halves, not split grounds: the ADS1299, its inputs and the ±2.5 V supplies sit on the left; the ESP32, USB-C, charger and buck on the right, over the same ground plane.

Noise sources far from the inputs: the buck and the charge pump are at the bottom edge, about 25 mm from the electrode connector at the top-left. The ESP32's antenna sits at the top-right corner with a keep-out on every layer.

Capacitors at the pins: 1 µF + 0.1 µF at every ADS1299 power pin (supply pairs under the chip on the bottom, as on the Cyton, but with short vias), 10 µF at each regulator output, VREF and VCAP capacitors beside their pins on the top. See the overview site for the videos.
