The Cyton and Daisy, explained

A 3D explorer, a 29-minute beginner course and short deep dives that show how the board works inside, from battery to brain waves. Made while learning the Cyton from its public schematics, datasheets and firmware, in the hope that it saves the next person some time.

How this was made. The 3D model is built from OpenBCI's published V4 placement files, outline and layer plots (and the Daisy's own files). Part data comes from OpenBCI's rev C and Daisy bills of materials. The ADS1299 figures come from Texas Instruments' datasheet SBAS499C, and the facts about start-up and lead-off were checked against the open-source Cyton firmware.

The videos and the explorer were made with the help of an AI assistant (Claude), and the narration is an AI voice (ElevenLabs). Mistakes are possible. If you spot one, please open an issue and it will be fixed.
